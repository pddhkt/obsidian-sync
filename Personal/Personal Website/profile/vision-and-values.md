# Vision and Values

Use this note to define what the website should express beyond a job history.

## Draft themes from the current experience

### Build practical systems

Work across software, product, and engineering has a common thread: turning a real operational need into a usable system.

### Learn across disciplines

The path from structural engineering to software development is not a reset. It is evidence of being able to learn complex domains and apply engineering judgment in new contexts.

### Carry ideas into execution

Startup and product work should show the ability to move between idea shaping, UI/UX, implementation, cloud infrastructure, and iteration.

## Prompts to answer in your own words

- What problems do you enjoy solving?
- What do you want to be known for in three years?
- Which kind of people or teams do you want to work with?
- What do you believe makes software useful?
- Which lessons from civil engineering still influence your software work?
- Which topics do you want to explore publicly?
