# Personal Website

> Build a personal website from reusable source notes: who I am, what I have done, what I am learning, and what I want to contribute next.

## What this workspace is for

This folder is the working system behind the website. It separates factual source material from public-facing versions so the same experience can support:

- A personal website and portfolio.
- Blog posts and social media posts.
- Topic research and brainstorming.
- Project descriptions for different audiences.
- A role-specific CV without rewriting work history from scratch.

## Recommended first pass

1. Answer the priority questions in [[Personal/Personal Website/open-questions]].
2. Edit [[Personal/Personal Website/profile/master-profile]] until the summary sounds like you.
3. Choose two or three public case studies in [[Personal/Personal Website/projects/_index]].
4. Use [[Personal/Personal Website/website-direction]] to settle the first website sitemap.
5. Capture the first topic in [[Personal/Personal Website/content/research-inbox/_readme|Research inbox]].
6. Use [[Personal/Personal Website/cv/cv-generation-workflow]] when a specific job opportunity appears.

> [!warning] Private material
> The CV source PDFs and [[Personal/Personal Website/profile/contact-private|private contact note]] are source material. Do not publish the entire workspace as a public website repository.
