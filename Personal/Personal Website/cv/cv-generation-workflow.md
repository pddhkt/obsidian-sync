# CV Generation Workflow

## Goal

Create a focused CV for a specific role without rewriting your complete work history every time.

## Step 1: Capture the opportunity

Create a note in `versions/` from [[Personal/Personal Website/cv/versions/cv-version-template|CV version template]].

Record:

- Target role.
- Company.
- Job description.
- Required skills.
- Useful keywords.
- What the employer appears to value most.

## Step 2: Choose the positioning

Select one clear headline and summary from [[Personal/Personal Website/profile/master-profile|Master profile]].

Examples:

- Frontend Developer.
- Product-minded Frontend Developer.
- Full-stack Product Engineer.
- Developer with startup and enterprise delivery experience.

Do not try to optimize for every role in one CV.

## Step 3: Select evidence

Use:

- [[Personal/Personal Website/experience/_index|Experience index]] for role history.
- [[Personal/Personal Website/projects/_index|Projects index]] for selected proof.

Prefer the bullets that match the opportunity. Keep older or less relevant experience compact.

## Step 4: Check every bullet

Each strong bullet should explain at least two of:

- What you built.
- Who used it.
- Why it mattered.
- Your ownership.
- The technology.
- The result.

Add numbers only when they are accurate and supportable.

## Step 5: Export and record

- Store the CV-specific note in `versions/`.
- Export the final PDF beside that note or in a matching subfolder.
- Add a row to [[Personal/Personal Website/cv/_index#CV versions|CV versions]].
- Keep private contact details out of any public website repository.
