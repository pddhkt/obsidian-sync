# Website Direction

## Purpose

The website should represent more than a CV. It should show the connection between:

- Software engineering experience.
- Product thinking and startup execution.
- Civil and structural engineering background.
- Personal ideas, research, and long-term vision.
- A habit of turning ambiguous problems into working systems.

## Working positioning

> I am a developer and builder with an engineering background. I work across product design, frontend and full-stack implementation, cloud systems, and practical problem solving.

This is a starting point. Rewrite it after answering [[Personal/Personal Website/open-questions#Positioning]].

## Primary audiences

Choose the first audience before designing the home page:

| Audience | What they need to understand quickly | Useful proof |
|---|---|---|
| Employers and hiring managers | Role fit, technical skills, delivery experience, and growth direction. | CV, selected projects, work history, technical posts |
| Potential clients | Problems you can solve and the quality of your execution. | Case studies, service areas, clear contact path |
| Potential collaborators | Your interests, product ideas, and working style. | Vision, app ideas, build notes, experiments |
| Professional network | What you are learning and thinking about. | Blog, social posts, short notes |

## Content principles

1. Show evidence, not only skill lists.
2. Explain your decisions, constraints, and results.
3. Publish a small number of useful posts consistently.
4. Keep private company and client details out of public pages unless publication is approved.
5. Use the Obsidian workspace as source material; publish only selected notes.

## Design direction to decide

- Tone: minimal professional, technical notebook, personal studio, or a mix.
- Language: English only, bilingual English and Traditional Chinese, or separate language versions.
- Visual style: restrained resume-like layout or a more expressive personal brand.
- Build approach: static site generated from selected Markdown, a CMS-backed website, or a custom application.
- Public contact method: email, LinkedIn, contact form, or selected channels.
