## One-Line Idea

A Setlog-like daily logging app for people and their pets, combining daily photo/video memories, auto-generated vlog exports, group sharing, and Strava-style pet walking activities.

## Raw Concept

Users record moments with their pet during the day through photos, videos, walks, and activity posts. At the end of the day, the app can combine those media clips into a vlog-style video that can be exported to social media, saved as a video, or shared as a story-like post inside the app.

The app also supports pet walking activities, similar to a workout app. Users can track walks with their pet, save the route, duration, location, distance, and attached photos, then decide whether each activity is public, shared with friends, shared with a group, or private.

## Main User Value

- Build a daily memory timeline with a pet.
- Turn normal daily photos and videos into shareable vlog content.
- Track how often and how far the user walks with their pet.
- Share pet activities with friends, groups, or the public.
- Keep private moments private while still supporting social discovery.
- Let dog walkers record proof, routes, photos, and recap videos for pets they walk for other owners.
