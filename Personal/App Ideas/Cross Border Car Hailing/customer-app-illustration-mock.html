Customer app mock

Illustrations inside the booking experience

One-page visual mock showing how transparent decorative artwork can support the cross-border car hailing app without covering the functional booking UI.

Design intent

The artwork should make the app feel professional, cross-border, and traveler-friendly. It should appear as a transparent layer, not as a rectangular image block.

1
Functional screens stay clear Patterns sit in unused space at low opacity. Forms, fares, and driver choices remain dominant.
2
Spot art marks emotional moments Use larger illustrations for welcome, confirmation, empty states, and support moments.
3
Travel cues stay abstract Use route lines, MPV, skyline, bridge, luggage, and payment motifs without promising a specific border path.
