# TimeKids — Project Home

> Audio-first history learning for young children: Timi narrates a short story, invites picture-based interaction, runs a gentle picture quiz, rewards the child with a sticker, and gives parents visible learning evidence.

## Current working direction

Current target: **private beta for 5-10 Hong Kong bilingual families**, not a full paid public launch.

The concept still has two possible go-to-market faces:

| Face | Audience | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Home face** | Parents and children | Build early traction, prove kids enjoy the loop, and create consumer proof before school pitches. |
| **Classroom face** | Kindergartens and teachers | Provide roster, lesson progress, quiz evidence, conversation summaries, and classroom-ready reporting. |

The shared core should stay the same either way: character engine, story content, bounded child-safe chat, quiz/reward loop, transcript storage, and summary/reporting data.

## Key unresolved decision

> [!question]
> Timi is a simple hamster-style mascot, and story navigation happens through Timi's hamster house/playground. Next decision: run the one-week Rive vs Lottie/dotLottie spike inside the real app stack and lock the beta motion path.

Current decision: **B2C-first private beta with B2B-ready data spine**, using **one shared product core**. The first buyer is **HK bilingual parents with children aged 4–6 who already pay for enrichment or educational apps and have tablet access**. Private beta needs **exactly 16 screens/states**, **10 stories with Traditional Chinese/Cantonese + English included**, structured as **3 flagship stories plus 7 simpler complete stories**. Monetization starts with a free private beta, then a low-price founding parent plan after feedback.
