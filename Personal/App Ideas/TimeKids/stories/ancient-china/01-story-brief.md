# Ancient China — Story Brief

## Beta purpose

Ancient China is the second flagship story for the private beta. It reuses the proven
[[../ancient-egypt/01-story-brief|Ancient Egypt]] loop to prove Chinese-relevance content without
needing the polish of a paid-launch flagship.

## Learning goal

Children should understand that **ancient Chinese people were creative and worked together** — they
made clever things like paper, kites, and lanterns, and built the Great Wall.

The Great Wall can be the visual hook, but the story should not become only "a giant wall." The
clever-ideas-and-teamwork angle gives the story a simpler age-4-6 concept and supports clearer
picture interactions (kite, lantern, wall).

## Child promise

Timi takes the child to Ancient China, meets A-Yu near a lantern-lit town, and asks the child to
help spot important things: lantern, kite, the Great Wall, paper, and people working together.

## Main characters

| Character | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Timi | Recurring bilingual time guide | Carries narration, picture prompts, bounded chat, quiz encouragement, and reward. |
| A-Yu | Local little inventor | Scripted side character who shows paper, kites, lanterns, and the Great Wall from a child-friendly point of view. |

|---|---|---|---|
| AC-V01 | 風箏 | kite | Colorful kite flying in the sky |
| AC-V02 | 燈籠 | lantern | Round red paper lantern |
| AC-V03 | 長城 | Great Wall | Long stone wall over green hills |

## Scene list

| Scene ID | Scene | Job |
|---|---|---|
| AC-S01 | Arrival (Village & Lanterns) | Establish Ancient China and the lantern-lit town. |
| AC-S02 | Meet A-Yu | Introduce local side character and clever-makers daily life. |
| AC-S03 | Great Wall Builders | Show people working together to build the Great Wall. |
| AC-S04 | Kites, Lanterns, and Paper | Let the child spot kites/lanterns and reinforce the creativity concept. |
| AC-S05 | Wrap-Up | Return to Timi's house with the reward and simple memory. |

## Reward

| Reward ID | Name | Visual |
|---|---|---|
| AC-R01 | Kite Sticker | A small friendly colorful paper kite, with a tiny Timi star badge. |

## Beta constraints

- 4-5 scenes only.
- 2 mid-story picture interactions.
- 2-3 bounded Timi chat turns.
- 3 picture quiz questions.
- No required reading.
- Scripted fallback must work without AI.
- No war, weapons, battles, or scary dragons; the Great Wall is cheerful teamwork.
- All selected art must be referenced from [[03-scenes-and-hotspots]].
