# Ancient Egypt — Story Brief

## Beta purpose

Ancient Egypt is the first flagship story for the private beta. It should prove the full TimeKids loop without needing the polish of a paid-launch flagship.

## Learning goal

Children should understand that **the Nile helped people, animals, boats, and daily life in Ancient Egypt**.

Pyramids can be the visual hook, but the story should not become only "pyramids and treasure." The river gives the story a simpler age-4-6 concept and supports clearer picture interactions.

## Child promise

Timi takes the child to Ancient Egypt, meets Nila near the Nile, and asks the child to help spot important things: river, boat, animal, pyramid, and people working together.

## Main characters

| Character | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Timi | Recurring bilingual time guide | Carries narration, picture prompts, bounded chat, quiz encouragement, and reward. |
| Nila | Local Nile helper | Scripted side character who shows river life, boats, and pyramids from a child-friendly point of view. |

## Beta constraints

- 4-5 scenes only.
- 2 mid-story picture interactions.
- 2-3 bounded Timi chat turns.
- 3 picture quiz questions.
- No required reading.
- Scripted fallback must work without AI.
