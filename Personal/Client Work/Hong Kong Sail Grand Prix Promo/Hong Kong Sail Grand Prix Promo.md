## Research snapshot

- Event/date/location: Hong Kong Sail Grand Prix is reported as the opening event of the 2027 SailGP season, scheduled for 2027-01-23 to 2027-01-24 at Victoria Harbour.

## Early creative territory

- Core idea: from Hong Kong's peaks to Victoria Harbour, the city itself becomes the race course.
- Tone: fast, cinematic, local, physical; less like a generic tourism postcard.
- Possible motif: 攀山涉水 as literal movement from trail ridge to shoreline to harbour racing.
- Risk: 10 seconds cannot carry too many activities. Better to choose 2-3 instantly readable Hong Kong images and make the SailGP reveal clean.

## Locked scene direction

### Scene 1: Mountain / ridge opener

- Low side-tracking shot of a trail runner or hiker moving along a Dragon's Back or Lion Rock-style ridge.
- Vertical 9:16 framing: runner centered lower-middle, ridge line leading upward, sea/skyline stacked behind.
- Golden hour / early morning.
- Sea plus distant Hong Kong skyline visible.
- Camera tracks fast, then lifts slightly toward ridge/coastline.
- Purpose: establish 攀山 and Hong Kong outdoor energy.

### Scene 2: Mountain to water transition

- Runner footstep impact match-cuts into kayak paddle hitting water.
- Sharp physical cut, not crossfade.
- Purpose: convert 攀山 into 涉水 instantly.

### Scene 3: Water activity

- Tight paddle splash, then wider left-to-right kayak tracking shot.
- Vertical 9:16 framing: paddle/splash in lower third, kayaker centered, coastline stacked behind.
- Sai Kung-style clear water, green islands, rocky coastline.
- Purpose: establish 涉水 while keeping forward momentum toward the harbour.

### Scene 4: Victoria Harbour build-up

- Low fast glide over Victoria Harbour water toward skyline.
- Vertical 9:16 framing: water texture in lower half, skyline rising in upper half.
- Central / TST visible.
- Purpose: transform the harbour into the race stage.

### Scene 5: SailGP / F50 reveal

- F50-style foiling catamaran crosses left-to-right across Victoria Harbour.
- Vertical 9:16 framing: catamaran crosses through middle third, mast and skyline kept inside frame.
- Low close-to-water camera, skyline behind.
- Spray and foil lift visible.
- Purpose: event payoff without over-explaining SailGP.

### Scene 6: Final event card

- Clean dark navy / deep harbour-blue card.
- Text: Hong Kong Sail Grand Prix / Victoria Harbour \| 2027.
- Vertical 9:16 safe area: keep title centered, away from top profile UI and bottom caption/buttons.
- Official logo, exact date, or website only if required by client.
- Purpose: instant event recognition.

## Proposed generation workflow

1. Gather and approve references per scene.
2. Use GPT Image 2 to generate one or more still frames per scene, using references only for direction.
3. Review stills for continuity: color, camera direction, skyline realism, water motion, and SailGP credibility.
4. Test Seedance in two ways:
   - Full 10-second pass from the scene-direction prompt and approved keyframes.
   - Separate scene clips if full-pass quality loses control.
5. Assemble the stronger output path, with final end card added cleanly in edit if needed.

## Pricing / generation strategy research

Search date: 2026-06-23.

### Public pricing facts found

- Seedance 2.0 supports direct generation from 4 to 15 seconds, with native 480p/720p output, and supports up to 9 image references, 3 video clips, and 3 audio clips in its open-platform description. Source: [Seedance 2.0 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14148).
- Replicate lists ByteDance Seedance 1 Pro and Seedance 1 Lite as supporting 5s or 10s video generation. Pro supports 480p/1080p; Lite supports 480p/720p. Sources: [Replicate Seedance 1 Pro](https://replicate.com/bytedance/seedance-1-pro), [Replicate Seedance 1 Lite](https://replicate.com/bytedance/seedance-1-lite).
- Replicate billing is generally pay-as-you-go. Public models are often billed by active processing time, while some models are billed by input/output; Replicate says cost estimates live on each model page. Source: [Replicate pricing](https://replicate.com/pricing), [Replicate billing docs](https://replicate.com/docs/topics/billing).
- Replicate's pricing examples for video models show output-second billing on some models, e.g. Wan 2.1 image-to-video examples at USD 0.09/s for 480p and USD 0.25/s for 720p. This is not Seedance pricing, but it is a useful public benchmark for output-second video billing. Source: [Replicate pricing](https://replicate.com/pricing).
- Publicly indexed Seedance 2.0 Chinese sources mention 1080p API token pricing around RMB 31 / million tokens with video input or RMB 51 / million tokens without input. This does not map cleanly to a per-10-second cost without provider token accounting. Source: [Seedance 2.0 Chinese summary](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seedance_2.0).

### Cost logic for this project

Assumption: Instagram Reels final is 10 seconds; final card can be built manually, so AI video generation likely covers Scenes 1-5.

| Strategy | Likely generated duration | Relative cost logic | Control |
|---|---:|---|---|
| One full 10s generation | 10s | Cheapest baseline: 1 full run. | Lowest control; one bad scene can ruin the whole run. |
| Scene-by-scene, Seedance 2.0 min 4s | 5 AI clips x 4s = 20s minimum | About 2x the output duration of one 10s run, before retries. | Higher control. |
| Scene-by-scene, 5s/10s provider such as Seedance 1 Pro/Lite on Replicate | 5 AI clips x 5s = 25s minimum | About 2.5x the output duration of one 10s run, before retries. | Higher control. |
| Scene-by-scene including generated title card | 6 clips x 5s = 30s minimum on 5s providers | About 3x the output duration of one 10s run, before retries. | Usually unnecessary because title card should be edited manually. |
| Hybrid | One 10s run + targeted 1-2 scene reruns | Usually cheaper than full scene-by-scene if the full run is usable. | Balanced. |

### Recommendation

Use a hybrid testing strategy:

1. Run one full 10-second generation using the approved stills as references if the chosen tool supports enough image references.
2. Review continuity, Scene 2 match cut, Scene 5 F50 credibility, and title-card readability.
3. Regenerate only failed scenes as separate clips.
4. Build the final title card manually in edit rather than generating it as a video scene.

Reason: scene-by-scene is likely 2-3x the raw generation cost because of minimum clip durations, but one full 10s pass has high failure risk. Hybrid gives a cheaper first read while preserving control for the hardest scenes.
