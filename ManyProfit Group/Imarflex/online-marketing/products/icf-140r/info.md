## Positioning

Use ICF-140R for content about:

- 冷風機 ≠ 冷氣
- 局部 / 個人範圍降溫
- 通風、較乾爽環境先最合適
- 水箱、冰晶 / 冰塊、蜂巢水簾、濾網保養
- 2 年保養登記和用後打理

## Visual Truth

Prompt or QA must preserve:

- Slim upright white tower cooler body.
- Black top control panel and dark front grille.
- Tall vertical vent/slat structure.
- White faceted side panels.
- Blue water/filter cues when showing mechanism or filter.
- Wheels/casters at base.
- Black remote.

Avoid:

- Air-conditioner unit.
- Sealed whole-room cooling scenes.
- Frost blast / ice explosion graphics.
- Fake UI text, fake price, fake model label, or unconfirmed performance guarantee.

## Claims Notes

- Frame cooling as local / personal-zone cooling.
- Do not claim it replaces air conditioning.
- Any cooling-degree figures must be marked as public reference / non-guaranteed / environment-dependent.
- Specs involving filter replacement frequency or replacement SKU are pending client confirmation.
