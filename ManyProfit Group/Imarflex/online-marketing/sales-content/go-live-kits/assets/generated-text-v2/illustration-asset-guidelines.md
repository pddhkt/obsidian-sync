# Illustration Asset Guidelines - July Product Posts

This guideline is for **illustration assets inside the post**, not for turning the whole post into an illustrated scene.

The post can still use a product cutout, lifestyle photo, or clean product render. The illustration system supplies the supporting assets: airflow ribbons, icons, mini diagrams, heat/cool cues, product-detail insets, and callout modules.

## Core Direction

Use a clean social-card layout with product truth protected. Add illustration assets only where they clarify the message, make the post more saveable, or create a recurring campaign identity.

Avoid relying on frame numbers as the main visual identity. Let the recurring asset system carry the carousel:

- a blue airflow / cooling ribbon,
- custom icon chips,
- small room-map modules,
- feature micro-illustrations,
- product-detail callouts,
- warm/cool state cues.

## Asset Layers

| Layer | Role | Visual Weight | Use When |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Product hero | Real product reference, product cutout, or product-bound render | 40-70% | Any SKU-specific post where product trust matters |
| Primary motion asset | Airflow ribbon, cool mist path, circulation loop | 20-40% | Explaining movement, cooling, airflow, room circulation |
| Diagram module | Mini room map, before/after comparison, placement route panel | 15-35% | Teaching a principle or placement decision |
| Icon chips | Window, AC, diagonal route, quiet, remote, timer, filter, water tank | 5-15% | Summarising features or giving scanable choices |
| Micro illustration | Detachable grille, blade, remote, towel, filter, caster wheel | 5-20% | Showing a detail that the product photo cannot clearly show |
| State cue | Heat haze, cool gradient, sound wave, sparkle, water cue | 5-15% | Showing problem/benefit without more text |

Do not use every layer in one frame. Most posts should use one product hero, one primary asset, and one supporting asset group.

## Campaign Asset Library

| Asset | Description | Best Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Airflow ribbon | Translucent Cool Air blue ribbon with arrow head or soft direction taper | IFQ circulation, placement, whole-room air movement | Too many arrows, sci-fi glow, random loops with no physical path |

| Heat patch | Soft orange patch, heat squiggles, or faded warm haze | Hook/problem frames: `焗`, `冇對流`, hot sofa/window zone | Alarmist red, fire-like effect, dominant orange palette |
| Mini room map | Simplified top-down HK room diagram with window/sofa/TV/AC/product | Placement, airflow principle, before/after circulation | Full architectural drawing, labels inside map, unreadable tiny furniture |
| Icon chips | Rounded or circular icon-only modules | Feature rows, placement choices, CTA benefit recap | Text inside chips, generic emoji-like icons, too many chips |

## Asset Use By Frame Type

### Hook Frames

Use:

- one strong problem cue,
- one product hero/cutout,
- one primary motion asset.

Recommended assets:

- heat patch,
- airflow or cool mist ribbon,
- small room/environment hint.

Avoid:

- icon rows,
- technical diagrams,
- dense spec chips,
- frame numbers as decoration.

### Explanation Frames

Use:

- split comparison module,
- simple line/ribbon contrast,
- one product or silhouette per side.

Recommended assets:

- straight line for ordinary fan,
- looping ribbon for circulator,
- dotted neutral room background,
- small check/cross cues only if subtle.

Avoid:

- long labels inside the diagram,
- fake scientific meters,
- overcomplicated airflow physics.

### Placement Frames

Use:

- mini room-map module,
- product placement marker/cutout,
- route ribbon,
- icon chips for modes.

Recommended assets:

- window icon,
- diagonal arrow icon,
- snowflake/AC icon,
- top-wall window / left sofa / right TV / upper-right AC layout lock.

Avoid:

- full photorealistic room if the message is placement logic,
- random furniture layout,
- AC unit floating or sitting on furniture,
- text inside icon chips.

### Feature Proof Frames

Use:

- product-detail inset,
- feature icon,
- short callout arrow.

Recommended IFQ assets:

- detachable grille line asset,
- exactly 3-blade open-front visual,
- remote-control micro asset,
- quiet wave / DC comfort cue.

Recommended ICF assets:

- water tank cue,
- washable filter asset,
- caster/wheel asset,
- airflow-angle asset.

Avoid:

- invented internal structure,
- fake numbers,
- fake labels on product surface.

### CTA Frames

Use:

- clean product hero,
- small benefit icon row,
- subtle ribbon or soft background cue.

Recommended assets:

- remote icon,
- warranty/service badge if confirmed,
- feature icons already introduced earlier in the carousel.

Avoid:

- introducing new visual metaphors,
- busy room maps,
- heavy educational diagrams.

## Visual Rules

### Icons

- Use Heritage Blue stroke with optional Cool Air fill.
- Keep icons custom and appliance-specific.
- Icons should be readable at mobile thumbnail size.
- Avoid text inside icons; place text in the main layout if needed.

### Ribbons

- Use one ribbon family per product type:
  - IFQ: airflow ribbon, smooth loop, arrow/taper direction.
  - ICF: cool mist ribbon, softer edge, water/droplet cue.
- Ribbons should follow a believable physical path.
- Use fewer, clearer ribbons rather than many arrows.

### Mini Maps

- Use simplified top-down or light isometric room diagrams.
- Keep furniture schematic and legible.
- Use icon chips around the map rather than labels inside the map.
- For IFQ + AC scenes, always lock:
  - top wall: window,
  - left wall: sofa,
  - right wall: TV/cabinet,
  - upper-right wall: split AC,
  - lower-left floor: circulator.

### Product-Detail Insets

- Use when a product feature is too small to read in the hero.
- Use a clean white or Rice White card surface.
- Keep one detail per inset.
- For IFQ open-front assets, the blade count must be exactly 3.
- For ICF filter/tank assets, use client references when possible.

## Generation Strategy

| Asset Type | Best Production Method | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Exact icons | Local SVG/vector overlay | Keeps icons reusable and consistent across frames |
| Text and punctuation | Local overlay when exactness matters | Avoids Chinese punctuation drift from image generation |
| Airflow ribbons | Local vector if layout is fixed; generated if exploring composition | Vector gives consistency; generated gives mood exploration |
| Room-map modules | Local vector for final; generated for concept exploration | Final placement logic should be deterministic |
| Product hero | Product photo/reference-bound generation | Product trust depends on correct shape |
| Product-detail inset | Client reference or carefully product-bound generation | Avoids invented grille/filter/tank geometry |

## Showcase Formats

### Single-Card Format

One image carries one product-selling point. Use it for direct product selling, CTA, and FB feed images.

Best asset mix:

- product hero,
- one primary ribbon,
- three feature chips,
- optional detail inset.

### Combined-Story Card Format

One image combines several carousel roles into one card: mood image, product hero, and feature description.

Best asset mix:

- mood/problem panel,
- product panel,
- feature/detail panel,
- one ribbon connecting the panels.

Use this when the post should feel richer than a single product card but the channel only needs one image.

### Carousel Format

Multiple images unfold the selling story.

Best asset mix:

- one recurring asset family across all frames,
- each frame gets one main asset job,
- final frame reuses prior icon chips as the CTA benefit row.

## Prompt Clauses

Use these clauses when asking GPT-image or another image model to generate integrated social-card assets.

### Asset-System Clause

```text
Use a clean product social-card layout. The illustrations are supporting assets inside the post, not the whole scene style. Keep the product/photo area as the hero, then add a reusable illustration-asset system: blue airflow ribbon, icon-only chips, small room-map inset, heat/cool cue, and product-detail micro illustrations.
```

### No-Frame-Number Clause

```text
No visible frame number, no progress number, no numbered badge, and no large decorative numerals.
```

### IFQ Airflow Assets Clause

```text
Add one recurring Cool Air blue airflow ribbon that starts from the circulator and loops through the room. Add only relevant supporting assets: small window icon, diagonal-route icon, AC/snowflake icon, mini room-map inset, or warm orange heat patch. Do not turn the whole poster into an illustrated room scene.
```

### IFQ Product-Truth Clause

```text
If the product is shown accurately, it must be a compact white round-grille air circulator with spiral grille ribs, twin wood-tone support arms, rounded white base, and slim white remote. If blades are exposed, show exactly 3 white blades. Avoid generic pedestal fan, extra blades, fake product labels, or invented side/back geometry.
```

### ICF Cooling Assets Clause

```text
Add a soft Cool Air / water-mist ribbon for personal-zone cooling, plus relevant supporting assets: open-window icon, water-level strip, droplet icon, washable-filter icon, wheel/caster micro illustration. Keep the scene ventilated and local-use; do not imply sealed-room air-conditioning replacement.
```

### Local Overlay Clause

```text
Keep text areas clean and simple. If exact Chinese punctuation matters, leave clean space for deterministic local text overlay instead of relying on generated typography.
```

## Production Recommendation

For future production, split the work into two asset types:

1. **Generated master images** for product/lifestyle background and overall mood.
2. **Deterministic overlay assets** for exact icons, ribbons, text, and diagrams.

This avoids asking the image model to solve everything at once. GPT-image can explore composition and mood, while local overlays protect typography, punctuation, icon consistency, and product-specific visual logic.

## Client Asset Requests

Ask the client for product images based on the asset needs:

| Need | Client Reference Needed |
|---|---|
| Product hero | Front, front 3/4, side if placement angle is needed |
| Product cutout | Clean white-background product photo, high resolution |
| Cleaning proof | Open-front, detached grille, exposed blade, cloth-cleaning shot |
| Detail inset | Remote, base/control panel, grille, blade, support arms |
| ICF water/filter proof | Water tank, filter, rear/side access, wheels/casters |
| Placement/angle scenes | Side/back product views if the room map or lifestyle scene shows side/rear geometry |

If those images are missing, keep product views front-safe and use illustration assets to explain the concept instead of inventing hidden geometry.
