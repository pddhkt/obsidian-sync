# IFQ-22R Direct-Sell Feature Showcase Example

This example demonstrates a post type that sells the product directly, not just a tips/topic angle.

## Selling Angle

**One compact circulator that makes the room air move better, stays comfortable for long use, and is easier to clean.**

Core product claims must stay within confirmed campaign/product information:

- IFQ-22R.
- DC quiet / energy-saving positioning from existing prompt work.
- 全方位對流 / room air movement.
- Easy detachable front grille.
- Remote and 2-year warranty where campaign copy allows.

Avoid:

- fake price,
- fake QR/URL,
- fake performance numbers,
- fake model labels,
- invented side/back product geometry,
- exposed blade scenes unless exactly 3 blades are visible.

## Format A - Single Card

Use when a single feed image needs to sell the product quickly.

Exact text direction:

```text
IFQ-22R
一部，帶動全屋空氣
DC 靜音・全方位對流・易拆前網罩
```

Composition:

- Product hero on the right or center.
- Large headline block on the left.
- Blue airflow ribbon behind or around the product.
- Three feature icon chips: quiet, circulation, easy-clean.
- One small grille/detail inset if there is enough space.

Asset mix:

- product hero,
- airflow ribbon,
- feature icon chips,
- optional product-detail inset.

## Format B - Combined-Story Card

Use when one image should feel like a mini carousel.

Exact text direction:

```text
IFQ-22R
由焗住，到成屋空氣郁起嚟
靜音・遙控・易拆前網罩
```

Composition:

- Left/mood panel: compact HK room with subtle heat patch.
- Center/product panel: IFQ product cutout or product-bound hero.
- Right/detail panel: three feature chips and a grille/remote inset.
- One airflow ribbon crosses mood panel -> product panel -> feature panel.

Asset mix:

- mood panel,
- product hero,
- heat patch,
- airflow ribbon,
- feature icon chips,
- detail inset.

This is useful when the post needs the feeling of a carousel but should be consumed as one image.
