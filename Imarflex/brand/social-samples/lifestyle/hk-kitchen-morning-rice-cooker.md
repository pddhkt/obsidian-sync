## Use

Lifestyle reference for a Hong Kong home morning scene. Good for communicating practical everyday use without relying on a stock-style model shot.

## Prompt

Realistic Hong Kong apartment kitchen morning scene with a compact rice cooker being used for breakfast prep, small bright kitchen, tiled backsplash, city window light, everyday bowls and utensils, partial hands only, natural editorial lifestyle photography, no visible brand logo, no text, no watermark.
