## Cadence story ("same promise, upgraded engine")

1. **Continuity** — the 4-blogs/month promise stands (A1, SEO backbone).
2. **Multiplier** — each monthly topic is now a weekly campaign kit: 1 blog → 3–4 social variants on a Mon–Fri rhythm. June proof: 4 campaigns → 15 posts. The old line「一次內容，三個 channel 同步用」literally cashed out.
3. **Why weekly beats monthly** — (a) constant feed presence vs bursts; (b) weekly signal-driven topic confirmation catches festival peaks / heatwave surges in the same week; (c) built-in discipline (A4 cap, post-card governance) — not "post more for the sake of it".
4. **Visual swap** — the month-scale Gantt retires because the delivery unit is now the week: calendar with weekly dots replaces the bar chart.
