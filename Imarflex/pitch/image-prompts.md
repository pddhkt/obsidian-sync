# Image Prompts — Imarflex Methodology Pitch Deck

5 new images to generate via GPT-image + 3 existing images to reuse. All slot into specific pitch deck slides. Brand voice:**可靠 / 溫暖 / 專業 / 貼地 / 日式精緻**.

> [!important] Shared style language — apply to all 5 prompts
> - **Medium:** editorial photography, magazine-quality (think *Kinfolk* / *Monocle* / *Casa Brutus*), not stock photography
> - **Lighting:** soft natural daylight from a side window, late morning or golden hour, warm but not amber-orange
> - **Color palette:** warm neutrals — Clay Beige (#E7D7C1), Rice White (#F7F6F2), Heritage Blue (#1E4B7A) accent only where natural (a kettle, a window frame), Steam Grey (#DCE1E6) cool neutral, Soft Charcoal (#3C3F42) accents
> - **Composition:** Japanese minimalist, generous negative space, rule of thirds, shallow-to-medium depth of field
> - **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 horizontal (landscape) for slides
> - **Mood:** intentional, lived-in, calm; not staged, not glossy, not "stock-photo perfect"
> - **Avoid:** handshakes, generic suited businesspeople, headsets, oversaturated colors, fish-eye, cluttered scenes, AI-typical glow effects, fake-looking food, hands holding smartphones in obvious way, "smart kitchen" sci-fi tropes

## Image 1 — Cover slide hero

**Slot:** Slide 1 of 17 (Cover)
**Purpose:** Set Imarflex brand tone for the entire deck. Heritage + craft + HK domestic context in one frame.

**Prompt:**

> Editorial photograph, 16:9 horizontal landscape orientation. A clean, sunlit Hong Kong kitchen countertop in early morning. Centred: a single Japanese-style stainless-steel rice cooker, side-angle three-quarter view, lid closed, with subtle steam rising softly from the vent. Beside it, a small ceramic bowl with raw rice grains and a folded linen cloth in warm beige. Background blurred but suggesting a wood-grain cabinet and a window letting in pale morning light. The composition is uncluttered, with at least 40% negative space on the right side for typography overlay. Color palette: warm neutrals (cream, oat, soft charcoal), with one accent of deep navy blue in the cooker's badge or a small detail. Magazine-quality editorial style, similar to *Kinfolk* or *Casa Brutus*. Soft natural daylight from a side window, not harsh. No people in frame. Mood: calm, intentional, made-with-care, 50-year heritage.

**Where it goes in deck:**
- Slide 1 cover — full-bleed background with title typography overlaid on the right negative-space area
- Title overlay (added in PDF, NOT in the image):「Imarflex 伊瑪牌 — Online Marketing Methodology」+ date

## Image 2 — Step 0 (Market intelligence scan)

**Slot:** Slide 5 of 17 (Step 0 introduction)
**Purpose:** Convey "looking outward / scanning the market" without being a literal magnifying-glass cliché.

**Prompt:**

> Editorial photograph, 16:9 horizontal landscape. A wide HK domestic kitchen window in soft morning light, viewed from inside looking out. Outside the window: a slightly out-of-focus suggestion of dense Hong Kong residential towers — recognisable city density but not specific buildings, abstract enough to feel atmospheric. Inside foreground: a wooden window-side ledge with a single steaming ceramic teacup, a folded newspaper (text deliberately unreadable / blurred), and a small notebook with a fountain pen resting on it. The notebook is open but pages are blank. Composition: viewer's eye should travel from interior intimacy to exterior city scale. Color palette: warm cream interior tones, cool steel-grey hint of city outside, Heritage Blue accent on the fountain pen or teacup detail. Aspect ratio 16:9. Lighting: soft morning daylight from outside. Mood: thoughtful, observing, before-the-work-starts. No people in frame.

**Where it goes in deck:**
- Slide 5 — used as a 40% width image on the left, with Step 0 explanation text on right 60%

## Image 3 — Step 1 (Business focus / hero SKU decision)

**Slot:** Slide 7 of 17 (Business focus + hero scoring)
**Purpose:** Convey "intentional decision-making, choosing one hero from many options" — the discipline behind the 6-dim rubric and DQ rule.

**Prompt:**

> Editorial top-down (flat-lay) photograph, 16:9 horizontal landscape. A clean linen-covered table or workbench. Arranged in a row from left to right: five small ceramic bowls, each containing a different kind of grain or rice (white rice, brown rice, jasmine rice, sushi rice, glutinous rice — visually distinct but all rice-family). One bowl in the centre-left position is slightly larger and elevated on a small wooden riser, drawing the eye — this is the "chosen" hero. The remaining four bowls are positioned with deliberate spacing, not crowded. Wooden chopsticks rest beside the elevated bowl. Above the row: a small handwritten paper note (text unreadable / abstract, just suggesting calligraphy). Color palette: cream linen, warm wood tones, varied rice colours from pearl-white to amber to deep brown, Heritage Blue ink on the handwritten note. Composition: balanced asymmetry, generous negative space top and bottom. Lighting: soft overhead diffused light, magazine flat-lay style. Mood: deliberate selection, craft-focused, expert curation. No hands or people.

**Where it goes in deck:**
- Slide 7 — full-width banner across the top of the slide (top half), hero scoring rubric table below

## Image 4 — Step 4.5 (AEO + AI-search era)

**Slot:** Slide 10 of 17 (AEO format audit introduction)
**Purpose:** Bridge the "traditional craft" feel with the "AI-search era" technical reality. Avoid sci-fi clichés.

**Prompt:**

> Editorial photograph, 16:9 horizontal landscape. A close-up of a wooden writing desk in a calm domestic study. Top half of frame: a paper notebook open to a page showing handwritten Chinese characters arranged in a structured Q&A format — questions clearly distinguished from answers by indentation (text content abstract / partially obscured, just suggesting structure). Bottom half: a vintage-feeling but contemporary writing tool — a fountain pen with a deep navy barrel, resting on the notebook. To the side: a small ceramic cup of green tea, steam barely visible. Lighting: warm overhead lamp light combined with soft natural daylight from one side, creating gentle shadows that suggest "considered thinking." Color palette: warm wood, cream paper, Heritage Blue pen accent, jade-green tea, Soft Charcoal ink. Composition: tight shot, ~70% negative space on right for text overlay. Aspect ratio 16:9. Mood: structured craft meets thoughtful writing — bridging tradition and discipline. No people, no devices, no screens.

**Where it goes in deck:**
- Slide 10 — left 40% image, right 60% AEO 5-axis rubric explanation

**Note on bridging:** This image deliberately AVOIDS computers / phones / "AI" sci-fi imagery. The metaphor is that AEO is a *writing discipline* (structured Q&A, source citation, careful framing) that translates equally to AI search and human readers.

## Image 5 — Closing slide / partnership

**Slot:** Slide 17 of 17 (Next steps / contact)
**Purpose:** Convey forward-looking partnership without using cliché handshakes or "team meeting" tropes.

**Prompt:**

> Editorial photograph, 16:9 horizontal landscape. A warm domestic Hong Kong dining setting in late afternoon light. Centred on a wooden dining table: a single Japanese-style rice cooker (closed, similar in style to Image 1), with a small ceramic teapot beside it. Three empty ceramic bowls and three sets of wooden chopsticks arranged around the table — set for three, but no one is yet seated. The plates are slightly turned, suggesting people are about to arrive. Behind the table: a wooden chair pulled out invitingly, and an out-of-focus suggestion of a window with late afternoon golden light. The composition implies anticipation — a meal not yet started, but everything ready. Color palette: warm wood, cream ceramics, Heritage Blue accent on the teapot or a folded napkin, golden hour light. Composition: medium shot, balanced, generous space above the table for typography. Lighting: warm late-afternoon side light, not harsh. Mood: ready, hospitable, anticipating arrival — the feeling of "let's begin together." No people in frame, but their absence is felt as expectation.

**Where it goes in deck:**
- Slide 17 — full-bleed background with closing text + contact details overlaid on upper third
