# Sales Purposes

Use these 6 sales purposes to classify why a post exists. The purpose should be measurable, not just descriptive.

## Sales purpose list

| Sales purpose | Meaning | Good KPI | Best-fit topic types |
|---|---|---|---|
| product-discovery | Help new customers notice a product/category. | Reach, saves, profile clicks, collection visits | recipe-use-case, product-proof, promotion |
| decision-confidence | Help customers choose and reduce hesitation. | Blog dwell time, PDP clicks, comparison clicks, WhatsApp enquiries | buying-guide, comparison, product-proof |
| direct-purchase | Push a specific product, offer, bundle, or seasonal campaign. | PDP clicks, add-to-cart, checkout, sales, ROAS | promotion, product-proof |
| trust-reduction | Reduce purchase risk and support anxiety. | Warranty clicks, parts-finder clicks, support-page visits, comment sentiment | trust-service, maintenance-how-to |
| lead-capture | Turn interested customers into WhatsApp, email, or account leads. | WhatsApp clicks, form submits, email signup, saved product | problem-solution, buying-guide, trust-service |
| retention-repeat | Keep existing customers engaged and create repeat/cross-sell. | Parts purchases, warranty registration, repeat purchase, email/WhatsApp engagement | maintenance-how-to, recipe-use-case, trust-service |

## Rule

If a post cannot name one primary sales purpose and one measurable KPI, it should stay in idea/backlog.
