# Production Volume Targets — 2026-06

This note defines how many signals, topics, post ideas, campaign topics, and final posts we should produce in the first sales-content cycle.

The rule is: gather more than we publish. A healthy month should start with 12-16 possible topic ideas, then narrow down to 4 campaign topics and a clear IG / FB posting plan.

## Monthly funnel

| Stage | Quantity target | What counts | Output location |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Market context bullets | 5-10 | Short external/context observations about season, category, competitor, trend, customer demand | [[online-marketing/business-focus/2026-q2-focus]] / research notes |
| Raw signals | 20-30 | Search queries, customer questions, competitor examples, product facts, seasonal triggers, promo/inventory notes | [[online-marketing/research-inbox/_readme]] |
| Raw topic ideas | 12-16 | Possible content topics before scoring | [[online-marketing/research-inbox/_readme]] |
| Scored candidate topics | 8-12 | Topics scored by business relevance, demand, purchase intent, fit, repurpose value, ranking chance | [[online-marketing/topic-research-workflow]] / research note |
| Approved campaign topics | 4 | One main topic per production week | [[social-post-plan-2026-06]] and campaign post records |
| Backlog topics | 4-8 | Good topics not used this month | [[online-marketing/research-inbox/_readme]] or next-month plan |
| Campaign bundle records | 4 | One campaign bundle for each approved weekly topic | [[posts/_readme]] |
| Blog / support pages | 4 | One main long-form or support destination per campaign | Blog / support notes |
| Social post records | 15 | Individual IG / FB / Story/Reel records | [[social-post-plan-2026-06]] and [[posts/_readme]] |
| Post-level ad candidates | 2-5 | Posts worth reviewing for boost/retargeting after tracking is ready | [[../_sales-content.base]] `Ad Candidates` view |
| Actual paid tests | 0-2 | Only launch if destination and tracking are ready | Meta Ads Manager / report |

## Minimum viable start

If time is tight, do not wait for all 20-30 raw signals before starting. The minimum viable first cycle is:

| Item | Minimum |
|---|---:|
| Raw topic ideas | 8 |
| Scored topics | 6 |
| Approved campaign topics | 4 |
| Blog / support outputs | 4 |
| Social posts | 12 |
| Backlog topics | 4 |

## Quality gate

A topic should not move from `candidate` to `approved campaign topic` unless it has:

- One clear product/service destination.
- One primary sales purpose.
- One measurable KPI.
- One IG angle.
- One FB angle.
- One CTA.
- Enough facts/images to produce without making claims we cannot support.
