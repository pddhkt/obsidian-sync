# Topic Types

Use these 8 topic types to classify every sales-content post. One post can have supporting angles, but it should have one primary `topic-type` in frontmatter.

## Topic type list

| Topic type | When to use | Example | Primary sales purpose | Recommended channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| buying-guide | Customer is choosing category/spec/size. | 電飯煲容量點揀? | decision-confidence | Blog, IG carousel, FB link post |
| comparison | Customer is comparing technology, product, or brand. | IH vs 普通電飯煲 | decision-confidence | Blog, IG carousel, Reel |
| problem-solution | Customer has a pain or question. | 電飯煲飯硬點算? | lead-capture | Blog, IG carousel, FAQ, WhatsApp |
| recipe-use-case | Show the product in a real life use case. | 氣炸鍋蝦多士 | product-discovery | Blog, Reel, IG carousel, Story |
| maintenance-how-to | Helps customers use, clean, or maintain products. | 風扇點拆洗? | trust-reduction | Blog, IG carousel, FB |
| product-proof | Explains why a product is worth buying. | 靜音風扇 dB / DC 馬達 / 搖擺角度 | direct-purchase | Reel, IG carousel, PDP section, FB |
| trust-service | Reduces risk around warranty, parts, delivery, support. | 2 年保養點登記? | trust-reduction | FB, Blog, FAQ, Story |
| promotion | Time-bound sales push. | 夏天風扇優惠 | direct-purchase | FB, IG, Story, paid ad |

## How to choose

| If the customer is asking... | Choose |
|---|---|
| Which one should I buy? | buying-guide |
| What is the difference? | comparison |
| How do I fix this problem? | problem-solution |
| What can I make with it? | recipe-use-case |
| How do I clean / maintain it? | maintenance-how-to |
| Why this product? | product-proof |
| Can I trust the brand / warranty / parts? | trust-service |
| Is there an offer now? | promotion |
