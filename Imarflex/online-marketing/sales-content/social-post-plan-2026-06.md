## What this adds beyond the original 4-post plan

The original plan produced 4 weekly blog-led campaigns. This plan makes the social layer explicit:

- Every IG / FB post has its own topic type and sales purpose.
- IG is not just "copy blog caption"; it is split into carousel, Reel, and Story.
- FB is used for link distribution and trust, not daily repeated product photos.
- Ads are attached to specific sales/trust posts only when tracking and destination pages are ready.
