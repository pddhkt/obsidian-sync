# First Sales Content Cycle Walkthrough — 2026-06

This note walks through one practical start-to-finish cycle: from finding topics, selecting posts, producing blog / IG / FB outputs, checking what is missing, and seeing what the end result should look like.

> [!important]
> Current date is 2026-06-04. The realistic plan is to use 2026-06-04 to 2026-06-05 for setup, then start the first full production week on 2026-06-08.

## Goal

Use one month to test whether sales-oriented content can move customers from useful information to product action.

Success does not mean "post every day." Success means the content creates measurable product movement:

- Blog / social content links to a clear product or service destination.
- IG / FB posts are useful enough to save, share, click, or ask about.
- The dashboard shows which topic types and sales purposes were used.
- At month end, we know what worked, what was missing, and what should be repeated.

## End-to-end flow

| Step | What happens | Quantity target | Output |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1. Pick business focus | Choose the product/category worth pushing this month. | 3-4 priority categories | Rice cooker, air fryer, fan, warranty/service |
| 2. Gather signals | Check search, customer, competitor, seasonal, and internal business signals. | 20-30 raw signals | 12-16 raw topic ideas |
| 3. Score topics | Pick topics that have business relevance, search/customer demand, purchase intent, and a destination. | 8-12 scored candidates | 4 approved topics + 4-8 backlog topics |
| 4. Create post cards | One Markdown note per campaign and per social post. | 4 campaign records + 15 social post records | Posts appear in [[../../../_sales-content.base]] |
| 5. Produce channel outputs | Blog, IG carousel/Reel, FB post, Story, optional ad. | 4 blog/support + 15 social posts | Draft-ready assets |
| 6. Publish and track | Add URLs, UTM, KPI notes. | 19 published/logged records if all outputs go live | Published post cards + performance data |
| 7. Review | Compare result vs assumption. | 1 report + 4-8 next-month topics | Next-month decision |

## What is missing before real publishing

| Missing item | Why it matters | Blocks publish? |
|---|---|---|
| Real PDP / collection / support URLs | Every content item needs a destination. | Yes |
| UTM naming rule | Needed to know which post drove traffic. | Yes |
| PostHog / GA4 / Meta Pixel events | Needed before serious ad spend. | Blocks ads, not organic |
| Google Search Console access | Needed to validate SEO topics. | No, but limits learning |
| Meta / IG / FB account access | Needed to schedule and read performance. | Yes for social |
| Product photos / approved images | Needed for product trust. | Yes for social / blog quality |
| Exact hero SKU, price, stock, margin | Needed before promotion or direct-purchase posts. | Yes for sales pushes |
| Author / reviewer identity | Needed for stronger SEO/AEO trust. | Not always, but important |
| Approval owner and review turnaround | Needed to publish weekly. | Yes |
| WhatsApp handling process | Needed if CTA sends customers to WhatsApp. | Yes for lead-capture |
| Ad budget and campaign owner | Needed for paid tests. | Blocks ads only |

## Go / wait decision

Start organic production if these are ready:

- Destination URLs
- Product facts
- Images or image plan
- Approval owner
- Social account access
- UTM rule

Start paid ads only if these are ready:

- Meta Pixel / conversion tracking
- UTM campaign naming
- Product destination pages
- Monthly ad budget
- Retargeting audience or clear sales campaign audience

## First-cycle judgement rule

Do not judge the whole strategy by one viral post. Judge the system by whether it creates a repeatable loop:

1. Topic chosen from business + customer/search signal.
2. Content produced across blog, IG, FB, and optional Reel.
3. Content links to a product/service destination.
4. Result is tracked in the post card.
5. Next topic improves based on the result.
