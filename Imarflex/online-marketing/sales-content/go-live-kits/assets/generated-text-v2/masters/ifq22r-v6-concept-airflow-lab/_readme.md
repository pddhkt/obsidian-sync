## Why This Exists

V5 kept the product front-safe, which helped product accuracy but reduced visual variety. V6 tests the opposite first: let the image model explore stronger composition, theme, and social-card energy using a fake unbranded white air-circulator placeholder. Product accuracy is not judged in this pass.

If a V6 direction is approved, the next pass must bind the selected layouts to real client product images:

- real IFQ front hero,

## Concept Rules

- Do **not** use real IFQ source images as product references.

- The placeholder may be inaccurate; judge only theme, hierarchy, layout, and social-card feel.

- Keep the style benchmark close to the pitch-deck social carousel: big numbers, strong Heritage Blue shapes, airflow diagrams, room fragments, icon chips, and saveable visual logic.
