## Visual Truth

Prompt or QA must preserve:

- White compact desktop circulator body.
- Round white spiral grille.
- Wood-tone circular center cap.
- Twin wood-tone vertical support arms.
- Rounded white base.
- Slim white remote.
- Exposed blade scenes must show exactly 3 white fan blades.

Avoid:

- Generic pedestal fan.
- Dark navy body.
- Five-blade or many-blade exposed fan.
- Fake logo badge, fake model text, fake prices, or invented spec stickers.
