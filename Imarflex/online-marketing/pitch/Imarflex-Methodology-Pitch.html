Step 1 · Business focus

One hero per category. Discipline of choice, not enthusiasm.

Two-level model: Quarter focus = thesis (categories + DTC moats). Monthly focus = bet placement (this month's promo, content, diff from quarter). Both anchored by 6-dim hero SKU rubric.

Quarter focus
Thesis (changes every 3 months)
Categories to push · hero SKUs per category · DTC moats · season arc · evergreen pillars. Output: yyyy-qx-focus.md
Monthly focus
Bet placement (changes monthly)
This month's actual promo · SKUs being pushed · content being shipped · diff from quarter reconciliation table · out of scope this month list (mandatory). Output: yyyy-mm-focus.md
Mandatory monthly sections Diff from quarter focus + Out of scope this month exist to catch silent-drop bugs. Example real case: Q2 focus pushed 抽濕機, monthly silently dropped it. Audit caught it. New rule enforces visible decision.

04 · Hard rules — enforced by the skill files, not just team norms

Three rules we won't break, even when asked.

Rules mirrored across multiple skills + agent files + persistent memory. Multi-layer enforcement means consistent behaviour session-to-session, agent-to-agent.

1
LIHKG / Reddit / 論壇 sentiment stays internal
Never enters brief, blog, PDP, social, pitch deck, or any client-facing deliverable. Aggregate sentiment only (eg「過去 90 日 N 條 thread 提及痛點 X」). Brand-self mentions (Imarflex) only — competitor brand sentiment never surfaces.

Why: Legal risk (quoting users without consent). E-E-A-T penalty (Google March 2026). Brand 信譽 risk if competitor sentiment used as marketing ammunition.
2
No fabricated citations or false schema facts
Every claim has URL + date + source. AEO auditor rejects content with "according to X report" but no URL. Schema declares only what PDP actually supports — no fake aggregateRating, no stale prices, no false stock status.

Why: Google penalty for false structured data. Brand 信譽. LLM hallucination amplifies false claims into AI-search answers — at scale.
3
Pitch-stage honesty — no lift commitments before measurement
At pitch stage we show methodology, not promises. ⚠️-heavy in scan output is normal (no client data yet). No "we'll lift your traffic X%" — we show how we'd measure, then measure honestly.

Why: Trust. Client can verify methodology rigour pre-engagement. Removes incentive to fabricate baselines.
