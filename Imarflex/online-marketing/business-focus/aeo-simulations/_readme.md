# aeo-simulations

AEO (AI-search) citation simulations: standardized-prompt baselines + monthly mention-rate deltas measuring Imarflex visibility across ChatGPT / Perplexity / Claude / Google AI Overviews.

## What lives here

- Per-run simulation notes recording which standardized prompts were tested and Imarflex's mention/citation rate per engine.
- Month-over-month deltas feeding the business-focus planning above.

## Do NOT put here

- Per-piece content scoring against the AEO rubric → `../../published-content/aeo-audits/`. This folder tracks *engine visibility over time*, not individual content audits.
