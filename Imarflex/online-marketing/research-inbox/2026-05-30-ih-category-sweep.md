- **Customer problem:** Buy too big → 飯硬 (insufficient water-to-rice ratio); buy too small → 不夠食 / 慳電不效
- **Possible keyword:** 電飯煲 容量 點揀 / 電飯煲 邊個容量好 / 點揀電飯煲 (high-volume parent)
- **Search intent:** Buying-guide, 决策中, high purchase intent
- **Destination / CTA:** IRC-20IH PDP + capacity comparison anchor + WhatsApp consultation
- **Evidence:** Q2 focus ✅ IH hero scoring + Trends compare data
- **Notes:** Funnels generic "電飯煲" searchers (~63 average daily score) into family-size argument. Highest priority per Q2 thesis.
