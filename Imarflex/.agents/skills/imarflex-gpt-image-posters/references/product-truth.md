# Imarflex Product Truth Reference

Use this reference before generating Imarflex social poster assets. Product geometry is part of the prompt, not an afterthought.

## Approved Visual Styles

| Style | Use | Visual Language |
|---|---|---|
| Muji-style minimal poster | Feature cards, hooks, CTA frames, checklist posters | Clean neutral background, generous white space, restrained sans-serif typography, simple product-first layout |
| Kinfolk/Monocle base | Lifestyle and editorial post frames | Natural window light, compact Hong Kong home context, practical props, magazine-like composition |

## IFQ-22R Air Circulator

Official campaign name: `"伊瑪牌"『龍卷』8.7吋全拆式遙控循環扇`.

Model: `IFQ-22R`.

Known look from reference images:

- White compact round-grille air circulator.
- Circular white front grille with spiral/radial grille ribs.
- Wood-tone circular center cap with Imarflex mark in product photo; do not invent new badge text.
- Exactly 3 white PP fan blades when the blade is exposed or detached.
- Two vertical wood-tone support arms.
- Pale grey rear yoke/bracket.
- Rounded white oval base with small touch buttons and dark IR receiver slot.
- Small slim white remote control may appear as a prop.

Prompt locks:

- Say "exactly three visible white fan blades" for any exposed blade, detached blade, cleaning, or rear/open-grille frame.
- Say "white round spiral grille, twin wood-tone support arms, rounded white base" for product hero frames.
- Avoid "extra blades", "five-blade fan", "ordinary pedestal fan", "dark navy body", "fake product label", and "generic desk fan".

## ICF-140R Evaporative Cooling Fan

Official campaign name: `"伊瑪牌" 可移式放霧遙控冰冷風機(14公升)ICF-140R`.

Model: `ICF-140R`.

Known look from reference images:

- Slim upright white tower cooler.
- Black/dark charcoal top control panel and front vent/grille.
- Tall dark front grille with vertical slats.
- White side panels with faceted/recessed surfaces.
- Blue water/filter cues in official product card and generated maintenance visuals.
- Large rear/side wheels and small front caster wheels.
- Black remote control may appear as a prop.

Confirmed campaign specs:

- 14L water tank.
- 3 wind speeds.
- Auto left-right oscillation and adjustable up-down airflow angle.
- 52 x 24cm washable filter.
- 2-year warranty.

Prompt locks:

- Keep it in ventilated personal-zone scenes: open window, desk corner, bedside/work corner, or local-use setup.
- Do not imply sealed-room whole-home cooling or air-conditioner replacement.
- Avoid air-conditioner units, frost blasts, ice explosion graphics, fake digital UI text, and unconfirmed performance guarantees.
